Sell ​​on the floor or create your own sales website? Compare to choose the right channel

Compare selling on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop with your own sales website: costs, customer data, brand, platform-dependent risks and how to combine the two channels effectively.

The e-commerce platform gives you a ready-made customer base, and your own website gives you the right to own your customers and your brand. The question is not one or the other, but what role each channel should play.

Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop help many sellers reach customers without having to build their own channels. So does a business that sells well on the stock marketplace need its own sales website? On the contrary, if you are just starting out, should you go public or build a website first? The answer depends on the product, profit margin, customer base and branding plan. The article compares the two channels in a practical way so that you can assign the correct role to each channel.

Selling on the floor: advantages and limitations

The biggest advantage of the floor is Guest lines are available: buyers are already there, used to paying and receiving goods, so a new shop can issue orders quite quickly. The floor also takes care of most of the infrastructure such as payment, shipping, and complaint handling. In return, businesses accept the following limitations:

  • Multi-class fees: fixed fee, payment fee, service fee, program participation fee. The fee schedule is decided by the floor and may change from time to time.
  • Compete on price: Your products are located next to dozens of other shops, customers can easily compare prices and switch to a cheaper shop.
  • Limited customer data: the buyer is a customer of the floor; Actively caring for and reselling them is limited by the platform's policies.
  • Algorithm and policy dependent: a change in display, fees or regulations can cause sales fluctuations that the shop has no control over.
  • Faded brand: Customers often remember which platform they bought on rather than remember the store name.

Own sales website: advantages and challenges

Own website is the channel wholly owned enterprise: domain name, interface, customer data, product presentation and sales policy are all decided by the business.

  • Master your data: know who the customer is, what to buy, and when to buy again, then proactively take care of and resell.
  • Build a brand: stories, images, and policies are presented in their own way, without being compared next to each other.
  • No floor fee per order, although there are still payment gateway fees, shipping fees and costs to attract customers to the website.
  • Suitable for wholesale, selling to businesses, high value products: transactions that require consultation, quotes, and capacity profiles that the floor does not support well.

The challenge is the website don't have guests. You must attract customers through SEO, advertising, content, social networks or old customer files, and must have people to operate: product updates, order processing, technical maintenance, and data tracking.

Don't have to choose just one: the hybrid model works

For many businesses, the logical way is Use the platform to get orders and use the website to retain customers. Some common combinations:

  1. Platform to find new customers, website to take care of old customers: the first order comes from the floor; future purchases, membership incentives, and new products introduced through the website.
  2. Website is the face of the brand: story, policy, capacity profile, agent system; Customers searching for the brand name on Google will see the main website.
  3. Website for wholesalers, agents, and businesses: price list by level, bulk orders, separate quotes, things the floor cannot do.
  4. Website welcomes visitors from content and SEO: articles on instructions, comparisons, and product selection advice that bring customers from Google straight to your channel.
It's okay to start small No need for a fully featured sales website on day one. You can start with the main product list, clear policies, place an order or send a quick WhatsApp message, then expand the shopping cart and pay online when customers return.

When should you invest in your own sales website?

  • Revenue on the floor has stabilized, but fees are increasingly eating into profits.
  • There are many buyers who want to proactively take care of them.
  • Sell products that require consultation, high value, or wholesale or sell to businesses.
  • I'm building my own brand, I want customers to remember the store name, not just the floor.
  • Want to reduce risk when all sales depend on one platform.

A legal note: websites with online ordering functions often must notify the Ministry of Industry and Trade according to e-commerce regulations, and also need to have full business information, return policies, shipping and security. Frequently asked questions

New to business, should I go public or build a website first?

If you need to place an order quickly for popular goods, the platform helps verify the product at a low initial cost. If you sell products that need advice, high value, or want to build a brand from the beginning, you should have a parallel website for customers to find and trust.

Can the sales website connect to the floor?

Depending on platform and scope. Synchronization of products, inventory or orders is possible if the platform provides the appropriate connection. This part needs to be unpacked and confirmed before quoting.

If I have a website, should I leave the marketplace?

Not necessarily. Many businesses keep both: a platform to reach new customers, a website to build a brand and take care of old customers. The proportion between the two channels should be adjusted according to actual data.

Where does a private website get customers from?

From Google search thanks to SEO, advertising, content on social networks, WhatsApp, email and existing customer files. The website needs to be operated and measured to know which channels bring in orders.

Do I have to notify the Ministry of Industry and Trade to create a sales website?

Websites with online ordering functions often must notify the Ministry of Industry and Trade according to e-commerce regulations. You should check current regulations or ask the website maker to prepare enough information.
