## Logo

The mark is a bold X built from two interlocking chevrons. The left stroke is cyan; the right stroke
is navy with an ice-cyan outline. The wordmark is set in Manrope ExtraBold with a wider, letterspaced
STUDIO line beneath it. The lockup is rebuilt at production resolution from the supplied master
artwork so it stays sharp in the header, footer, loader and app icons.

| Asset | Use |

## Typography

- **Wordmark:** Manrope ExtraBold, tight tracking.
- **STUDIO line:** Manrope SemiBold, wide tracking.
- **Interface and body:** Manrope, with system sans-serif fallbacks.
- **Display headings:** the site's bundled display face, used at tight tracking and large sizes.
